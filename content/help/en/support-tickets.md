## Submitting a ticket

From the Tickets screen, click **New Ticket**. You'll need a title and description, and can optionally set a priority (low, medium, high, or urgent — medium by default) and a category (Technical Support, Bug Report, Feature Request, Account Issue, Billing, General Inquiry, or Other). You can attach files by dragging them in or clicking to browse (up to 5MB per file).

## Tracking a ticket

Each ticket opens as a message thread — you and the HRInno support team can reply back and forth, and any attachments are downloadable from the thread. Updates appear in real time, so you don't need to refresh the page.

## Ticket status

Ticket status (e.g. marking a ticket resolved) is managed by the HRInno support team, not by company admins directly — as a company admin, you can view your tickets and reply, and the team will update the status as your request progresses.

## Who can access this

Any logged-in user can submit and view their own support tickets.
