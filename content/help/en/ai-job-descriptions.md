## How it works

While creating or editing a position, fill in the position name first, then use the "Generate with AI" option. This opens a small dialog where you can type a rough draft or a few notes about the role (at least 20 characters) — the AI expands this into a fuller job description you can use as a starting point.

Generating a description uses some of your company's AI credits, and the dialog shows a note about that before you generate.

## Using the result

Treat the generated text as a **first draft, not a final version**: review it, adjust tone and specifics to match your company voice, and correct anything that doesn't quite fit the role before publishing the position. The AI is a drafting aid to save you time, not a replacement for a final human read-through.

## Who can use this

Anyone who can create or edit a job position (admins and managers, depending on your company's setup) can use the AI description generator. It isn't restricted by plan.
