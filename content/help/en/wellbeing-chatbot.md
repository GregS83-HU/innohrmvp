## For employees

Open the wellbeing check-in screen and click **Start** to begin a short conversation with the AI chatbot about how you're doing. It's designed to be quick and low-pressure — a wellbeing pulse check, not a survey.

## For admins: the wellbeing dashboard

Admins see an aggregated dashboard with the average happiness score, participation numbers and trend, PERMA-model charts (radar/pie/bar breakdowns), and "areas for improvement" cards with suggested actions. You can filter the period shown (7/30/90/365 days) and refresh the data.

Individual employee responses are never shown — only aggregated, anonymized results.

## Plan availability

The AI wellbeing chatbot is included on **Momentum and Infinity** plans. It is **not included on Free**. If your company is on Free, employees will see a message that this feature isn't part of your current plan when they try to start a session — visit **Manage Subscription** from the account menu to see upgrade options.

Note that even on Momentum or Infinity, this feature (like payroll, time & attendance, absences, and performance) also requires your company to have completed onboarding — see Getting Started.
