## Creating a job posting

From the Positions area, click to create a new position. You'll need to fill in:

- **Position name**
- **Manager** responsible for the role (required — chosen from a dropdown)
- **Employment type** (full-time, part-time, contract, internship, or temporary)
- A **short description** and a **detailed description**
- **Start date**

Optional fields let you add a location and location type (onsite/hybrid/remote), a salary range with currency, and whether the salary should be shown publicly, plus an application deadline.

Once the position is created, you can also run an AI-assisted match against your existing candidate pool from the same screen (see below).

## The applicant pipeline

Candidates who apply are tracked per position. From a position's pipeline, admins and managers can review candidates, add comments, and move candidates through next steps. Access is scoped to your own company — you can only see candidates for positions your company owns.

## Matching candidates with AI

After creating a position, you can trigger an AI-assisted comparison between the position and your existing candidates, which scores and ranks them for you. This runs in the background with a progress indicator and can be skipped if you'd rather review candidates manually.

## Plan limits on open positions

The number of positions you can have **open at the same time** depends on your plan:

| Plan | Open job positions |
|---|---|
| Free | Up to 3 |
| Momentum | Up to 5 |
| Infinity | Up to 10 |

This limit applies only to *creating new* open positions — closing an existing position frees up a slot, and downgrading your plan never deletes or hides positions you already have; it only stops you from opening more beyond your new plan's limit.

If you reach your plan's limit, you'll see a message when trying to create a new position. Visit **Manage Subscription** from the account menu to see upgrade options.
