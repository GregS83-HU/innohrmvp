## Uploading a certificate

From the medical certificate upload screen, choose a file (PDF or image, up to 1MB), then manually enter the employee's name and the certificate's start/end dates. No AI or OCR service reads the document — the certificate is stored as-is, and its key details are typed in by whoever uploads it.

You can add a comment, then confirm to save it (or cancel to discard it).

## Viewing and downloading certificates

The certificate list lets you search, and toggle between showing all certificates or hiding ones already marked as treated. Each certificate has a checkbox to mark it treated and a comment option.

To **view** a single certificate, click the view (eye) icon — this generates a short-lived secure link and opens the file in a new tab. Links are generated on demand and expire quickly; there's no permanently public URL for any certificate.

To **download multiple certificates at once**, use the download screen: pick a date range and click Download to get a ZIP file containing an Excel summary plus each matching certificate file.

## Plan limits on uploads

The number of certificates you can upload **per month** depends on your plan:

| Plan | Medical certificate uploads / month |
|---|---|
| Free | Up to 5 |
| Momentum | Up to 10 |
| Infinity | Up to 20 |

If you reach your plan's monthly limit, you'll see a message when trying to upload — visit **Manage Subscription** from the account menu for upgrade options. Certificates you've already uploaded stay accessible regardless of plan changes; the limit only affects new uploads.

## Availability for new (self-serve) companies

Uploading a medical certificate is currently held behind the same onboarding-completion checkpoint as time & attendance, absences, performance, and the wellbeing chatbot (see Getting Started) — on any plan, including paid ones. If you try to upload before your company's onboarding call is complete, you'll see a message explaining this, with a link to get in touch.
