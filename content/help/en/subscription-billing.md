## Viewing your plan

The Subscription page (from the account menu, **Manage Subscription**) shows your company's current plan and status, along with your remaining AI credits.

## Subscribing or upgrading

The same page shows a comparison of the three plans — Free, Momentum, and Infinity — with a Subscribe button for each. Choosing a plan takes you to a secure Stripe checkout page to complete payment; once done, you're returned to HRInno on your new plan.

## Buying additional AI credits

Below the plan comparison, you'll find AI credit packs you can purchase separately if you're running low, also handled through a secure Stripe checkout page.

## What this page doesn't do yet

There is currently no self-service "manage billing" portal button on this page (for things like updating a card on file or viewing past invoices directly in HRInno). If you need to update billing details or have a billing question outside of subscribing to a plan, contact HRInno support (see the Support section of this guide).

## Who can access this

Managing your company's subscription is an admin-only action.
