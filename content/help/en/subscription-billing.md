## The plans

HRInno has three plans. Both paid plans are priced as a monthly base fee plus a fee per active employee, so your bill follows the size of your team.

| | Free | Core | Growth |
|---|---|---|---|
| Price | Free | 25,000 HUF/month + 1,000 HUF per employee | 40,000 HUF/month + 750 HUF per employee |
| Team size | No limit | Up to 30 employees | 31 employees and up |
| Open job positions | Up to 2 | Up to 5 | Up to 10 |
| Medical certificate uploads / month | Up to 5 | Up to 10 | Up to 20 |
| AI credits / month | 50 | 100 | 250 |
| Time & attendance and absences | Locked preview | Included | Included |
| Performance management | Locked preview | Not included | Included |
| AI wellbeing chatbot | Not included | Not included | Included |
| Advanced recruitment reporting | Locked preview | Not included | Included |
| New support tickets | Not included | Included | Included |

Annual billing is available on Core and Growth at **15% off**, paid upfront.

Time & attendance, absences, performance management, the wellbeing chatbot, and medical certificate uploads also unlock only once your company has finished its account setup and its first payment has gone through (see Getting Started).

## Viewing your plan

The Subscription page (from the account menu, **Manage Subscription**) shows your company's current plan and status, what you're paying based on your current number of employees, and your remaining AI credits.

## Subscribing

New companies usually subscribe through the **Finish setup** steps (see Getting Started), which recommend the right plan for your team size. You can also subscribe from the Subscription page: pick monthly or annual billing, then click **Subscribe** on Core or Growth. You'll be taken to a secure Stripe checkout page to pay by card; once done, you're returned to HRInno on your new plan.

The plan you can choose depends on how many active employees you have: Core is available up to 30 employees, and Growth from 31. Add your employees first if you're planning to subscribe to Growth.

Optionally, tick **assisted onboarding call** before checking out (a one-time 60,000 HUF fee, added to your first invoice). Our team will then help you set up HRInno. Your modules unlock right away either way.

## Your bill when your team changes

When you add, deactivate, or reactivate an employee, the per-employee part of your subscription is updated automatically, and Stripe adjusts your next invoice for the difference. This works the same way on monthly and annual billing.

## Managing billing and changing plans

Once you're subscribed, the Subscription page shows a **Manage billing** button. It opens Stripe's secure billing portal, where you can update your card and view past invoices. Switching between Core and Growth, or between monthly and annual billing, isn't available directly on the Subscription page yet; use the billing portal or contact HRInno support (see the Support section of this guide).

Canceling or downgrading never deletes your data. Everything you already have stays accessible; plan limits only apply to creating new items.

## Buying additional AI credits

Below the plan comparison, you'll find AI credit packs you can buy separately if you're running low on your monthly allowance, also handled through a secure Stripe checkout page.
