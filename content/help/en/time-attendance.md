## For employees

The time clock screen has two tabs:

- **Clock In/Out**: a large button to clock in, which switches to a clock-out button once you're active, along with a live counter of time worked. Below that, you'll see a weekly summary — hours worked, on-time days, and any overtime.
- **History**: your past time entries, each tagged with a status badge (on-time, late, or overtime).

## For managers

Managers have a separate team view showing time-clock activity across their team, so you don't need to check each employee's history individually.

## Plan limits and availability

Like absences, time & attendance is included on Momentum (up to 20 employees) and Infinity (up to 100 employees) — on Free, it's a locked preview only. It also requires your company's onboarding call to be complete before use, on any plan (see Getting Started).
