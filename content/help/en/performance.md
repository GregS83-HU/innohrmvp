## Goals

Your performance dashboard shows quick stats (active goals, goals needing a pulse update, red flags, and anything pending approval) and a list of your active goals, each with a status dot (green/yellow/red).

To create one, click **New Goal** and fill in a title, description, and success criteria. New goals start as pending approval until your manager approves them.

## Pulse check-ins

Each week, goals that need an update prompt you with a "Pulse reminder" banner linking to the pulse check-in screen. There, for each goal needing an update, you pick a status (green/yellow/red), add a progress comment, and — only if you mark the goal red — describe the blockers.

## Team performance (managers)

Managers see a team dashboard with Overview, Red Flags, and Pending Approval tabs, stat cards per employee, and an Approve action for goals awaiting sign-off.

## Who can access this

Like payroll, time & attendance, and absences, performance management isn't restricted by plan tier — every plan has identical access once your company has completed onboarding (see Getting Started).
