## Views

Payroll has two ways to work with employee pay:

- **List view**: a straightforward list of employee payroll records, with its own Export button.
- **Grid view**: an editable grid where you can update fields like position, department, and salary directly in the table. Rows expand to show allowance and deduction detail, and a "Bulk Actions" button lets you apply changes to several employees at once.

Use **Add Employee Payroll** to set up payroll for an employee who doesn't have a record yet.

## Allowances and deductions

Opening an employee's payroll record (from either view) lets you manage their allowances and deductions in a dedicated editor alongside their base compensation.

## Closing a pay period

Period status (open, closed, or reopened) and the "Close This Period" action live inside the payroll export screen — open Export from List View, and you'll see the current period's status along with the option to close it.

## Exporting payroll data

Use the **Export** button in **List View** to generate a payroll export. (Note: the Export button inside Grid View is not yet wired up — use List View's Export for a working export.)

## Plan limits and availability

Payroll is an admin-facing module. It's included on Momentum (up to 20 employees) and Infinity (up to 100 employees) — on Free, it's visible as a locked preview only, not usable for real payroll data. Like time & attendance and absences, it also requires your company's onboarding call to be complete before use, on any plan (see Getting Started).
